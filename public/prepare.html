BEFORE YOU MEET THE OCEAN

Make a little space for the water.

A gentle invitation to prepare for your personal Ocean Portal experience.

You do not need to know what will happen. You do not need to perform anything perfectly. Come as you are, with a little time, an open heart and the intention you wish to bring to the Ocean.

01 · Find a quiet moment

Choose a moment when you can pause without rushing. You may sit beside the sea, near a bowl of water, or simply somewhere you feel comfortable. A few undisturbed minutes are enough.

02 · Remember your intention

Bring to mind the area of life you selected and the question or intention you shared. You do not need to force an answer. Let your intention be simple, honest and your own.

03 · Connect with water

If you wish, hold a little water in your hands or take a slow sip. Feel its temperature. Listen to your breath. Allow the water to remind you that life is always moving.

04 · Choose how you wish to receive

Your Ocean Portal experience is personal. You may receive its message quietly, through reflection, or simply by allowing yourself to be curious. There is no required ritual you must perform at home.

You are not expected to do anything perfectly. Your presence and your intention are enough.

The Ocean will meet you where you are.

When you feel ready, return to your personal Ocean journey.
